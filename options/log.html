Activity log

Every request to a tracking company Strict Switch saw, and what happened to it. Blocked and allowed are real trackers. Category off is everything in a category you've switched off; with the default settings that's content like videos, images and fonts, which sites usually need. Repeats are folded into one line with a count. The log keeps the last 3,000 entries, is cleared when the browser closes, and shows addresses without their query strings.

Reports include your settings and the entries matching the current filter (up to the newest 500), plus your settings changes. They list sites you've visited, so check before sharing. Report a problem copies the report and opens a GitHub issue form for you to paste it into; nothing is sent by the extension.
