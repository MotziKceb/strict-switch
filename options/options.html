Strict Switch

Strict tracker blocking that works alongside your browser's own protection. Step any site down to Balanced or Basic, or lock it down, from the toolbar button.

Strict layer

Tracker categories to block on every site that isn't stepped down.

    Sites with their own settings

    Lockdown (Ctrl+click Strict in the popup) blocks every known tracker, including content. Balanced opens the categories below for companies you visit regularly. Basic turns the Strict layer off. Exceptions are trackers you've allowed on one site from the popup. Subdomains are included.

      The categories Balanced lets through from companies you visit regularly. Social and Tracking content are the ones that tend to break sites; ads, analytics and the rest stay blocked unless you add them here.

        Companies you visit regularly

        On Balanced sites, these companies' trackers in the categories Balanced opens are let through, similar to Microsoft Edge's Balanced mode. Click Block to keep a company's trackers blocked anyway. A company is listed once you've visited its sites at least 3 times in the last 30 days, counted from your browsing history on this device.
