Strict Switch

Strict-level tracker blocking on top of Edge's own tracking prevention. Step any site down to Balanced or Basic from the toolbar button.

Set Edge to Basic

This extension provides the Strict layer, so set Edge's own tracking prevention to Basic. Every site then keeps Edge's Basic protection, even the ones you step down.

No built-in safety net

This browser has no tracking prevention of its own, so sites you put on Basic get no tracker blocking at all. Blocking third-party cookies in the browser's settings gives them some protection.

    Sites stepped down

    Balanced blocks trackers except those from companies you visit regularly. Basic turns the Strict layer off, leaving Edge's own protection. Subdomains are included.

      Companies you visit regularly

      On Balanced sites, trackers from these companies are allowed, like Edge's own Balanced mode. Click Block to keep a company's trackers blocked anyway. A company is listed once you've visited its sites at least 3 times in the last 30 days, counted from your browsing history on this device. Nothing leaves your device.
